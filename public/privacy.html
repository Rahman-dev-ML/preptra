Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

PrepTra (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is an independent educational application that helps users prepare for Pakistan’s CSS examination. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

1. Summary

PrepTra does not require an account to use core study features. We do not sell personal information. We do not use the app to build advertising profiles.

2. Information we collect

In the current version of PrepTra:

  • No account data: We do not ask you to create an account or provide a name, password, or profile.
  • No intentional personal data collection: The app is designed so that we do not collect names, phone numbers, contacts, precise location, photos, or payment details through PrepTra.
  • Technical / usage signals: When you use PrepTra over the internet, standard technical information may be processed automatically by hosting providers (for example IP address, device/browser type, and request timestamps) to deliver pages, past papers, and other content securely and reliably.

3. How information is used

Technical information processed by infrastructure providers is used only to:

  • Deliver and operate the PrepTra website and app content
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and diagnose outages
  • Improve reliability and performance

4. Third-party services

PrepTra content may be hosted and delivered through providers such as Vercel and content or CDN partners needed to serve files (for example PDFs). Those providers process technical data under their own privacy terms solely to provide hosting and delivery.

PrepTra is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC) or the Government of Pakistan.

5. AI features

Some practice features may use automated systems to generate or explain questions. Do not submit sensitive personal information into any free-text fields. AI outputs may contain errors and should be verified against reliable sources.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for cross-app advertising.

7. Data retention

Because PrepTra does not maintain user accounts, we do not keep personal study profiles. Hosting and security logs may be retained by infrastructure providers for a limited period according to their operational policies.

8. Children’s privacy

PrepTra is an educational tool intended for CSS aspirants and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your choices

You can stop using PrepTra at any time by uninstalling the app or leaving the website. If you believe we hold personal information about you and want it deleted, contact us using the email below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of PrepTra after an update means you accept the revised policy.
